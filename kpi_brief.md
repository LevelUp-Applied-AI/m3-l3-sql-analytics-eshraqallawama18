# KPI Brief — Levant Tech Solutions


## KPI 1 — Department Salary Efficiency
**Definition:** Total salary expenditure per department ÷ number of employees in the department  
**Current Value:** 162,500 (example from Q2)  
**Interpretation:** Shows how much the company spends on average per employee in each department; higher numbers indicate departments with more senior or high-paid staff.

---

## KPI 2 — Project Staffing Ratio
**Definition:** Number of assigned employees ÷ number of projects  
**Current Value:** 3 employees / 2 projects = 1.5 (example from Q4)  
**Interpretation:** Indicates project coverage; higher ratio suggests projects are well-staffed.

---

## KPI 3 — Employee Utilization Rate
**Definition:** % of employees assigned to at least one project  
**Current Value:** 80% (example from Q7)  
**Interpretation:** Shows how effectively employees are allocated to projects; low utilization may indicate idle staff.
